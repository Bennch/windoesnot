## Connectivity applications

The separately versioned [WinDoesNot Bluetooth](https://github.com/Bennch/windoesnot-bluetooth)
fork is based on [Overskride](https://github.com/kaii-lb/overskride), copyright kaii,
under GPL-3.0-or-later. [WinDoesNot Network](https://github.com/Bennch/windoesnot-network)
is based on [Adwaita Network](https://github.com/PlayRood32/adw-network), copyright
its upstream contributors, under GPL-3.0-or-later. Both retain the upstream
license and credits. Exact commits are recorded in `sources.json`; the image
build compiles these sources against its own GTK libraries.

## Graphics control application

[WinDoesNot Graphics](https://github.com/Bennch/windoesnot-graphics) is based on
[LACT](https://github.com/ilya-zlobintsev/LACT), copyright Ilya Zlobintsev and its
contributors, under the MIT license; the license text ships with the package
(`/usr/share/licenses/windoesnot-graphics/LICENSE`). The exact upstream and fork commits
are recorded in `sources.json`. [WinDoesNot Audio](https://github.com/Bennch/windoesnot-audio)
is based on [Easy Effects](https://github.com/wwmm/easyeffects) (GPL-3.0-or-later) and keeps
its upstream license and credits.

## Service and app logos in the installer

The icons of web apps and AI tools in the installer's app list (`iso/software/icons/`) are taken from
[Dashboard Icons](https://github.com/homarr-labs/dashboard-icons) (Apache-2.0) and scaled to 64 px; the app icons of
Flatpak apps come from the Flathub catalogue. The logos and names shown (for example Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+,
YouTube, WhatsApp, ChatGPT, Claude, Ollama) are trademarks of their respective owners and are used only to identify
the service the entry opens. WinDoesNot is not affiliated with or endorsed by these companies.
